# Question-first redesign

Built-in image generation, no reference images. Earlier visual concepts are set aside, not deleted. One post is presented as a visual question with two facets; both choices open the preserved original text, not a rewritten response. No poll or vote collection is implied. Illustration/photos are conceptual and not part of the supplied JSON. PC and mobile share the same content.

Use case: ui-mockup. Create ONE outstanding, radically fresh Persian RTL responsive WEBSITE DESIGN presentation showing matching PC and mobile screenshots together, wide landscape 1792x1024. Large desktop screenshot occupies left 70%, portrait mobile screenshot right 25%, both full and straight-on with fine outlines. Target boys aged 17–20. Educational/religious informational content with ONE recommended post at a time. User has rejected previous card feeds, neon photo banners, 3D portals, clouds, arches, glassmorphism and magical scenes. Start from a completely different UX and art direction.
CONCEPT: "TWO SIDES OF A QUESTION" — an interactive photographic editorial screen, a question-first visual experience. Contemporary independent youth magazine meets an interactive decision page. Brilliant clean warm white #FAFAF6 with ink black typography, shocking vermilion #FF4F32 and a little safety-yellow. Flat graphic interface, angular cutouts, sharp clean rules, zero shadows, zero cards, zero rounded content containers, no sidebar, no fantasy, no dark-glow UI. Typography is a dominant design element, huge elegant condensed-bold Persian with correct connected RTL glyphs.
CONTENT exact source question "تقوا؛ محدودیت یا مراقبت؟" forms the only large heading. Interpret this visually through two contrasting young-life editorial photographs and a choice, not a paragraph. These are illustrative analogies, not actual events from the source.
DESKTOP screenshot: tiny temporary wordmark "مطالب" at top right, only search and profile outline icons at top left. Massive two-line Persian title in upper section "تقوا؛ محدودیت" then "یا مراقبت؟", left-aligned only if RTL legibility stays correct, intentional whitespace. Below a large unusual offset diptych occupying 60–65% viewport: one unframed crisp black-and-white closeup photo of a locked urban gate and fence with a warm red graphic line suggesting a barrier; beside it a beautiful rich-color documentary-style photograph of a young Iranian male about 19 fastening a skateboard helmet, casual t-shirt, natural confident smile, urban skatepark, warm daylight, realistic NOT famous face and no branding. The second photo represents caring for yourself, not an unsafe stunt. The two photos have DIFFERENT angular geometric crops, one tall one wide; editorial asymmetry, cutout red vertical strip between them. This is one post split into two facets, NOT multiple post tiles.
Only two highly visible selectable words at bottom of the photos: "محدودیت" and "مراقبت", drawn as bold flat text actions with arrow icons and red underline. Small explanatory interface action "انتخاب کن، بخوان" may be beneath but no more text. Both choices open the same ORIGINAL article, no opinion counts, voting percentages, polls or generated answer. Small bookmark/share/comment icons in thin footer. Lower corner "سؤال بعدی" subtle navigation selects another single post independently, no sequence or completion.
MOBILE: SAME white/black/red interface, same exact Persian question at top, matching photograph subjects in a tightly art-directed asymmetrical diptych below occupying 60% of mobile, helmet youth the primary color focal point and locked gate smaller inset NOT a card. Same two labeled options "محدودیت", "مراقبت" in accessible bottom hit areas. Tiny top logo and search; bookmark/share icons and swipe-next cue. No article text visible before choosing. Less than 20 Persian words per screen.
Visual effects come from surprising composition, stark typographic scale, graphic red rule, mix of monochrome and color photography, controlled image masking, NO neon/glass/3D. No decorative religious calligraphy, books, mosque scenery, portal, mountain sky, gaming UI, collaged lorem ipsum or outside presentation labels. No engagement statistics, dates, followers, episode number, collection names, grids or reading progress. This must look like a coherent, implementable, editorial interaction design, not a marketing poster. Precise pixel-aligned realistic web UI and exceptionally sharp polished imagery.
