# Thought portal concept

Built-in image generation. Fresh responsive PC/mobile concept, no input references. A single suggested post becomes a surreal 3D scene. Source title: دوباره می‌شود بال درآورد.. . The scene is illustrative. Full text opens on explicit action; reduced motion and ordinary keyboard navigation remain implementation requirements.

Use case: ui-mockup. Create ONE extraordinary yet usable responsive Persian RTL educational/religious single-post discovery WEBSITE concept showing PC and mobile together in a landscape image about 1792x1024. Desktop screenshot occupies left 70%, full mobile screenshot right 25%, same concept and same post, straight-on, minimalist outlines, no actual hardware. Audience boys aged 17–20. The user explicitly wants radically different, out-of-the-box design. Do NOT repeat white content cards, conventional social feed rows, right sidebars, dark neon photo banners, glass card dashboards, bookshelves or collection grids.
CREATIVE DIRECTION: "THOUGHT PORTAL" — a playful surreal spatial canvas, like entering a visually striking idea. Rich ultramarine/indigo solid background with soft lilac atmospheric gradients, radiant butter-yellow and coral details. One enormous freestanding rounded ARCHWAY rendered in striking editorial 3D, placed in a spacious canvas. Inside the archway is a surreal visual representing the real source post "دوباره می‌شود بال درآورد..": vivid coral-orange paper-like stairs rise through pale clouds toward a huge yellow sun, a small young adult male silhouette around 19 in casual clothing with backpack at the entrance, blue sky and a few floating sculptural feathers. Perspective and depth are arresting, beautiful tactile clay-and-paper material, premium creative art direction, no literal angels, saint depictions, mosque cliché, photos, buildings from earlier concepts or religious symbols. The portal is the ONE suggested post, not a button leading to a library. This illustration is metaphorical hope, not source evidence.
DESKTOP UI: no conventional navbar block; tiny temporary wordmark "مطالب" top right, search icon and profile top left. A few curved colored graphic dots and fine orbital guide lines around central portal give spatial navigation feel, but no graph/analytics or decorative text. Large bold gorgeous correctly connected Persian title "دوباره می‌شود بال درآورد.." placed as 2 short lines beside/below portal with great readability, only small chip "امید". Floating small cream capsule button "وارد شو" opens full post. Around lower area three tactile round buttons bookmark, comment and share with accessible icon shapes, no counters. Two small lower-corner browsing controls, left "پیشنهاد بعدی" with right-facing directional symbol and right a shuffle icon; only one post visible, no previous episode sequence. A tiny thematic preview is allowed as a distant blurred sculpture, not a second card. Layout must look like an actual responsive browser product, not an art poster.
MOBILE: same ultra-blue spatial canvas and SAME archway/stair/sun scene, adapted vertically to dominate 70% screen. Title near lower third large and legible, small topic chip, "وارد شو" capsule, bookmark/comment/share row and subtle upward-swipe cue. Tiny header wordmark and minimal bottom nav icons. No dense text, article paragraphs, quote text, collection names, episode numbers, progress, dates, fake likes, login wall, slogans or external presentation titles. LESS THAN 20 Persian words per screen. Show desktop and mobile as clearly matched screens. Core visual effects: real 3D portal depth, soft material shadows, atmospheric cloud layering, tactile floating controls, unexpected asymmetry. Avoid neon glow everywhere and excessive decorative items. Make it jaw-dropping, imaginative, sophisticated, optimistic, spacious and actually readable for youth, with image/illustration carrying most of the storytelling.
